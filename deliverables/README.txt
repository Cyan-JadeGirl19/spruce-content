DESIGN SYSTEM (why it looks better than before)
-----------------------------------------------
- Both companies' ACTUAL logos used throughout (winter-white variant of the
  Spruce Lighting wordmark for dark backgrounds; official white Spruce Pro mark).
- Exact brand palette extracted from the logos: Deep Teal #00303C,
  Cyan #1CC8D0, Lime #90D000, Spruce Red #C82028 + C9 bulb accent colors.
- Signature devices: logo sparkles, C9 bulb strands, cyan/lime accent rule,
  red urgency chips, lime CTA pills with soft shadow.
- Typography: Poppins (ExtraBold headlines / Medium subtext) — matches the
  geometric wordmark.
- Spruce Pro assets feature the company's REAL job photos (crew, aerial,
  stone wash, windows, gutters) pulled from sprucepro.com, plus a
  digitally simulated "before grime" state for transformation posts.
- Spruce Lights assets feature cinematic AI-generated holiday photography
  (no text baked into photos — all type is real, crisp and editable).
